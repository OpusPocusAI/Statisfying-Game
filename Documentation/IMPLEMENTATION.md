## Palms and wet sand — November 16

This round follows up the independent review: all three reviewers flagged aliased, shimmering palm fronds and a missing wet-sand band.
- **Anti-aliasing.** The game rendered with none: MSAA was off in the PC render pipeline asset and the camera had no post anti-aliasing, so every thin edge crawled. `Assets/Settings/PC_RPAsset.asset` now uses 4x MSAA. Average frame time is 11.2 ms (was 11.1), p95 11.4 ms.
- **Palm fronds.** `tropical_plants.py` builds each palm frond as one broad blade per side (`blade()`): the leaflets are fused into a web for the inner half of their length, then split into 13 bold, drooping teeth. This replaces 24 separate thin leaflets per side, so the crown reads as solid painted combs with a zig-zag edge. The young spear fronds keep the old pinnate shape. Each palm is about 8.4k triangles (was about 9k). `PalmsAndWetSandPass` re-imports `palm_0..2` in place, so every placed palm updates.
- **Wet sand.** In `PainterlyForward.hlsl`, the damp band on the wet-sand strip used to begin only where the strip bends down to the water. That slope is foreshortened from eye height and mostly under the swash, so the band barely showed. The band now starts on the level sand and is fully wet before the bend, a 3–4 m darker, warmer band with a wandering tide line, still zero at the strip's inland edge so no seam shows. Its sky reflection is weaker (0.28, was 0.5); the old value lifted the damp sand back to dry-sand brightness. Its sun glint is broader, though under the high sun the sheen still rarely shows.

Captures: `Captures/Review/palms-r1..r2` (ignored; compare b6 and b2 with `review-nov16`). Build, standalone verification and all 22 PlayMode tests pass. Not done: the sand points' wet margin uses the same band, unchanged in width; the broad and fern understory still uses thin pinnate leaflets, now softened by MSAA.
